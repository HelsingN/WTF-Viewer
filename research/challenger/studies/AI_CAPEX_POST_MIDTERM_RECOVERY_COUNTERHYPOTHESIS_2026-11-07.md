## 3. Competing falsifiable mechanisms

### H-RECOVERY — seasonal/political liquidity reset

1. Elections and new leadership raise risk premia and generate a concentrated repricing.
2. The election resolves one component of uncertainty; a subsequent VIX decline and falling credit spreads relieve selling pressure.
3. AI infrastructure contracted demand, revenue-producing capacity and executed financing remain functional despite lower equity multiples.
4. Broad market and semiconductor prices recover without persistent deterioration in project economic outcomes.

**Predictions to test, not facts:** price stabilizes and a *sustained* reclaim of the video author's S&P 500 7,600 reference occurs; semiconductor market breadth improves; credit spreads normalize; new issuer financials continue to show durable cash collection and funded capacity delivery. A rally alone does **not** validate favorable AI unit economics.

**Falsifiers:** multiple new independently confirmed R3/R5/R6/R7 deteriorations, measurable sustained cancellation/deferment of *funded* projects, or no rebound while macro uncertainty abates. Price changes cannot replace the specific economic evidence.

### H-REVERSAL — structural cash-flow and funding feedback

1. Excessive equity valuations, high compute expense, heavy contractual commitments and constrained power/permitting create a return-on-capital gap.
2. Funding costs or execution constraints propagate to economically funded projects (as opposed to speculative planned MW).
3. Observably lower realized compute economics, cancelled payments/commitments, or delayed/cancelled funded capacity damage the AI investment chain.
4. Semiconductor demand/financing responds with a lag; share-price decline may lead, coincide with, or *fail to predict* this mechanism.

**Confirming tests:** like-for-like issuer cost/revenue or project outcomes (R3); enforceable actual payment/cancellation changes (R5); lost/recovered billable capacity (R6); project-specific funded economic postponement/restructuring (R7). Record improvement evidence as rigorously as deterioration. **A ~12% one-day chip-index fall alone does not qualify.**

### H-INTERMEDIATE — valuation compression without investment contraction

Both H-RECOVERY and H-REVERSAL can partly fail: AI demand and funded buildout can stay high while financing margins, valuation multiples, or rate expectations contract. This outcome requires separate tracking; avoid false “bubble popped / bullish breakout” dichotomy.
