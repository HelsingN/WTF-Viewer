## Common acceptance rule

For every factor, identify primary evidence in scope, first-public date, baseline/new information, counterevidence, any concrete contradiction, chosen 0/1/2 or UNKNOWN, qualitative confidence, and plausible alternative. Stop when classification is defensible with disclosed uncertainty, not when every private field has become available. Use ASSESSED, ASSESSED_WITH_UNCERTAINTY, UNKNOWN or BLOCKED according to the merged evidence-sufficiency protocol. Escalate only an identifiable error, not generic incompleteness. P1–P4 stay separate. No overall Challenger score recalculation in this work package.

## Output and review gates

A. Complete P0 decisions first; B. complete P1 and P2; C. publish one factor-by-factor review and independent reviewer disposition; D. verify latest PR-head CI before merge. A factor is reopened only for material newly published evidence, identifiable source error or genuine contradiction.
