## 2. CRITICAL — Anthropic S-1/A: operating cash economics and enormous commitments

The amended filing reports **Q3 revenue $16.956bn** versus Q2 **$11.555bn** (about **+46.7% QoQ**, versus +144% in Q2). **Q3 operating loss $567m** versus **Q2 operating profit $974m**; **Q3 GAAP net loss $23.291bn**, down from **$66.309bn** Q2. GAAP net losses include material noncash convertible-instrument fair-value changes and MUST NOT be called cash burn. Nine-month revenue $33.242bn and net loss $104.039bn; cash, equivalents and short-term investments $131.845bn as of Sep 30.

**Purchase and lease commitments ~ $895.4bn** (as of/subsequent to Sep 30), substantially for compute/infrastructure, **$69.8bn due in the following 12 months** and **$447.5bn expected before end-2030**; remainder 2031–2045. These are scheduled commitments, not money already spent, nor a single one-year debt maturity. Compute spending increased to approximately **$9.9bn** in Q3 from **$5.6bn** in Q2; revenue per compute dollar reportedly fell from $2.08 to $1.72. Filing warns infrastructure counterparties depend on third-party financing; delayed or costlier credit could delay, reprice, reduce or prevent capacity delivery.

**Counterevidence:** revenue is growing rapidly; >11,900 customers each generating >=$100k trailing annual revenue versus >1,500 at end-2025; substantial liquidity. This is **CRITICAL financial/capacity counterparty risk**, not demonstrated widespread cancellation or physical oversupply. No formal IPO price or listing date in amended S-1. Do not assert $1.5tn valuation as company-set offer price.
